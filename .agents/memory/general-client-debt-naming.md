name: General-client debt naming
description: Approved neutral wording for combined overdrafts and unpaid fee assessments.
---

Use “Total owed” for the dashboard’s combined debt figure for all clients, including fee-only, non-fee, mixed and unknown-origin overdrafts. Keep the compact named account debt presentation.

**Why:** The user approved this naming policy because the former “Unpaid fees” figure includes all overdrafts, not just maintenance charges.

**How to apply:** Keep verified fee records labelled as fees in fee history. Never infer current fee debt from a negative balance or a historical fee charge alone. Preserve debt amounts, accepted contracts, access restrictions and the symbol-only GBP exception.
